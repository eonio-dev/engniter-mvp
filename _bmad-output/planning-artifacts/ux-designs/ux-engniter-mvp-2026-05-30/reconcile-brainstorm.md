# Brainstorm ↔ UX Spine Reconciliation

Only qualitative ideas or behavioral cues that appear in the brainstorming session but do not clearly survive in `DESIGN.md` or `EXPERIENCE.md`.

## Dropped qualitative ideas / behavioral cues

- **Misfit before polish** — The brainstorm emphasizes a product attitude of surfacing low-fit deals *before* teams invest in polished outputs. The UX spine keeps "fit criteria" visible, but not the stronger behavioral cue that the system should actively protect attention from bad-fit work.

- **Show movement, not just freshness** — The brainstorm frames updates as helping users see *what moved* in scope, risk, timeline, or assumptions. The experience spine preserves stale-state awareness, but not the richer comparative behavior of exposing negotiation movement rather than only marking an artifact stale.

- **Discovery acceleration, not just follow-up packaging** — The brainstorm positions the product as an active discovery accelerator that suggests the *next best questions* by impact on scope confidence. The UX spine preserves the Clarification Packet, but the more intentional behavioral cue of steering discovery quality is muted.

- **Value = avoided risk, not just speed** — The brainstorm repeatedly stresses that the product should feel valuable because it catches bad commitments, exposes unknowns, and prevents rework. The current UX spines strongly encode trust and caution, but they do not explicitly carry forward this "avoided risk is the win" evaluative posture.
